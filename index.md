## 3. Information we process

### 3.1 Information you provide

- Dog profiles (for example name, breed, size, age/life stage, real vs virtual)
- App mode (Prep / With my dog(s) / Adding another)
- Care completions, notes you enter, quiet-hours or manual weather overrides
- Soft-path / pavement-conditioning style preferences you set in the App

This information is stored **on your device** in the App's local database.

### 3.2 Information from your device (permissions)

**Location**  
If you grant location permission, the App reads your approximate or precise location so it can request weather for your area and support walk-related features. Location is used for that purpose; it is not used to build an advertising profile.

**Motion / steps / activity**  
If you grant the relevant permission, the App may read step or motion data **during a walk session** to help verify that a walk involved real walking (and not, for example, driving). Step data is processed on device for verification and local history in v1.

**Notifications**  
If you allow notifications, the App schedules local reminders for care tasks. Reminder content is generated on device.

**Photos / camera / contacts / microphone**  
Not used by the App's core v1 features described in this policy. If a future version requests them, this policy will be updated first.

### 3.3 Weather requests (third party)

To show heat/cold guidance, the App may send **latitude and longitude** (from your device, when permitted) to a weather provider.

- **Default provider:** [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) (typically no API key; request includes coordinates and returns temperature, humidity, wind, etc.)
- **Optional alternate provider:** If configured by the developer build, another weather API endpoint may be used. That provider would receive coordinates as needed to return weather.

Weather responses may be **cached on your device** for offline use. The App does not need your name or email to fetch weather.

Review the weather provider's own privacy terms for how they process API requests.

### 3.4 Information we do not collect in v1 (by design)

- Account registration / login
- Payment card data inside the App (donate, if ever enabled later, would use an external link and that provider's policy)
- Advertising identifiers for cross-app ads
- Selling or renting your personal information to data brokers

## 4. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Generate educational care schedules and readiness progress on your device
- Adjust walk guidance for weather and surface risk heuristics
- Verify walk sessions using step-aware checks
- Send local care reminders you opt into
- Improve reliability (for example caching last weather reading)

We do **not** use your dog-care data to train third-party advertising models.

## 6. Sharing

We share data only as follows:

1. **Weather provider** — coordinates (when you use weather features) as described above  
2. **Operating system / app stores** — Apple and Google process install, crash, and standard store analytics under their policies  
3. **Legal requirements** — if required by law or to protect rights and safety  

We do not sell personal information.

---

## 7. Retention

- Local App data remains on your device until you delete it in-App (if provided) or uninstall the App  
- Cached weather is kept only as long as useful for offline guidance, then overwritten  
- Uninstalling ReadyPup removes the App's local database from your device (subject to OS behavior)

Because v1 has no cloud account, we generally **cannot** remotely retrieve or restore your care history after uninstall.

---

## 8. Your choices and rights

You can:

- Deny or revoke **location**, **motion/fitness**, and **notification** permissions in system settings (some features will degrade or stop)
- Clear App data / uninstall to remove local records
- Email **anoop.pamu@gmail.com** to ask privacy questions

Depending on your location (for example EEA, UK, California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing, or to appeal a denial. Contact us at the email above. Because most ReadyPup data stays on your device, the fastest deletion method is usually clearing App data or uninstalling.
